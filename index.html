What pushes an Okara price up

  • Corner, boulevard or park-facing position
  • Street width — 40 ft beats 25 ft, every time
  • Gas connection already in place
  • Clear, transferable record with no dispute

What pulls it down

  • Undeveloped scheme with no possession date
  • Shared or disputed inheritance in the fard
  • No independent access road
  • Low-lying plot that takes monsoon water

Costs on top of the price

  • Stamp duty and registration fee
  • Capital value tax and TMA / district fees
  • FBR advance tax — s.236K buyer, s.236C seller
  • Society transfer fee and dealer commission
How it works

From first call to registry, in five steps

Punjab's land record is digitised now. That is good news — it just means the checks happen in a specific order. Here's the order we follow.

Tell us the brief

Area, size, budget and timeline. A five-minute call on WhatsApp saves three wasted visits.

See the shortlist

We show you three or four that genuinely fit, and tell you what's wrong with each one.

Check the record

Fard malkiat from the Arazi Record Centre, mutation history, society NOC and dues clearance — before any money moves.

Bayana & agreement

Token money against a written agreement on stamp paper, with the balance date and penalties spelled out.

Transfer & possession

Taxes paid, biometric verification done, sale deed registered, keys handed over. We're there for all of it.

Documents to have ready

  • CNIC of buyer and seller (and NICOP for overseas Pakistanis)
  • Original title document — registry, allotment letter or transfer letter
  • Fard malkiat / record of rights for the property
  • Society or TMA no-objection certificate and dues clearance
  • Approved building plan, for constructed property
  • Recent utility bills showing no arrears

Checks we never skip

  • Seller's name matches the record exactly — no verbal “my brother owns it”
  • No pending litigation, attachment or bank charge
  • Inheritance shares settled where the owner has passed away
  • Plot dimensions measured on site against the file
  • Access road is legally part of the scheme, not someone's land
  • Payment through traceable banking channels

Questions

Okara property, answered plainly

The questions we're asked on the phone most weeks.

How much does a 5 marla house cost in Okara?

As at August 2026, 5 marla houses in Okara typically ask between PKR 70 lakh and PKR 1 crore. Areas such as Saad City, Depalpur Road and Jawad Avenue sit in that band. Smaller 2–3 marla houses in Faisal Colony or Government Colony start around PKR 25 lakh. The final figure turns on street width, construction quality and whether gas is connected.

Which areas of Okara are best for investment?

For capital growth, buyers focus on the newer schemes along Canal Road and the Lahore–Okara Road side — Canal View, Model Town Okara and Gulberg City — where plots are still available at development-stage prices. For rental income, the older central areas around Company Bagh, Civil Lines and the Railway Road hold tenants best. Okara Cantt Civil Area is the premium end and holds value, but entry is expensive.

What is a marla, and how does it compare to a kanal or an acre?

In Punjab, 1 marla is 225 square feet (25 square yards). 20 marla make 1 kanal, which is 4,500 square feet. 8 kanal make 1 acre. So a 5 marla plot is 1,125 square feet and a 10 marla plot is 2,250 square feet.

What documents do I need to buy property in Okara?

You'll need your CNIC (or NICOP if you're an overseas Pakistani), and from the seller: the original title document — registry, allotment letter or transfer letter — the fard malkiat or record of rights, a no-objection certificate and dues clearance from the housing society or TMA, the approved building plan for constructed property, and recent clear utility bills.

We obtain the fard from the Arazi Record Centre and check the mutation history before any payment is made.

What taxes and fees are payable on a property transfer in Punjab?

Expect stamp duty, the registration fee, capital value tax, TMA or district fees, and FBR advance tax — section 236K on the buyer and section 236C on the seller, charged at different rates for filers and non-filers. Housing societies also charge their own transfer fee.

These rates change with each federal budget and provincial notification, so we confirm the exact amount for your property on the day rather than quoting a stale figure.

Do you deal in agricultural land near Okara?

Yes. Okara District is farming country — it is one of the largest potato-producing districts in Pakistan, and also grows maize, cotton and fodder. We deal in cultivable land along the chak roads and canal distributaries in all three tehsils: Okara, Depalpur and Renala Khurd. Land is priced per acre, and we verify the khasra numbers, the watercourse rights, the tube well and the access road before you commit.

Can overseas Pakistanis buy property in Okara through you?

Yes, and a good share of our work is exactly this. We send video walkthroughs and record checks over WhatsApp, deal with your nominated family member here, and can work with a registered power of attorney where you cannot travel. We insist on payment through banking channels and on you seeing the fard yourself before anything is signed.

How long does a property sale take in Okara?

Where the record is clean and both parties are ready, four to six weeks from bayana to registry is normal. Add time if the property is inherited and shares need settling, if a society NOC is slow, or if the seller is abroad and a power of attorney has to be attested. We tell you at the outset which of these applies to your file.

Do you charge a commission, and how much?

Yes — property dealing in Okara works on commission, and we agree it in writing before we start rather than raising it at the closing table. What we charge depends on whether it is a sale, a purchase or a rental, and on the value of the property. Ask us on the first call and you will get a straight number.

Which areas does Muhammad Properties cover?

All of Okara District. In the city that means Okara Cantt, Civil Lines, Company Bagh, Katchery Bazar, Government Colony, Faisal Colony, Model Town, Jawad Avenue, Canal View and Canal Road, Gulberg City, Al Kheer City, Al Rehman Town, Aziz Housing Scheme, Saad City, Haider Town, Mehboob Town and the GT Road belt. Outside the city we cover Depalpur, Basirpur, Haveli Lakha, Hujra Shah Muqeem, Renala Khurd and the surrounding chaks.

Do you handle rentals as well as sales?

Yes — family houses, upper portions, bachelor accommodation, offices and shops. We screen tenants, support police verification, and put the tenancy on stamp paper with the rent, increment and notice period written down. It costs a little more at the start and saves a great deal later.
